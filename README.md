One 1024×1024 PNG that sums up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026:
34 stories across 7 regions — COP31's no-deal fallout, tankers burning in the
Strait of Hormuz, sea drones hitting a Russian warship off Libya, NATO meeting
on the Russian missiles that fell in Poland, Democrats taking their biggest
House majority since 1992, Taiwan's market slide, Liverpool 4–0 Manchester
United, and more — with a stat strip, a region bar, a UTC timeline, three lead
cards, twelve small cards, a dotted world map with numbered pins, and a date
check box naming the other days' news that was deliberately left out.

Every story on the page happened, or was first reported, on Monday
23 November 2026.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the pre-rendered
`entry.png` shipped in this repository to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(default `/output/entry.png`), after checking the PNG magic bytes and the
1 MiB size limit. Both runs write identical bytes.
